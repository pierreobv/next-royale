Play together

Up to 8 players from anywhere, and bots fill the field up to 16 beans. One of you creates the game and runs the show.

Create a game

Choose Play online with friends, then Create a game. You get a room code like KQMT, and Copy invite link gives you a link that drops your friends straight into it.

Join a game

Open the invite link, pick the game under Live games, or type the 4-letter code. Then wait in the lobby for the show to start.

Talk while you play

Press T to chat, hold V to talk (the browser asks for your microphone the first time). Hold Q for the LinkedIn emotes, and press P for photo mode.

Good to know

The first game after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to connect while the game server wakes up. After that it's quick. Everyone plays the same version, so refresh the page if a game says yours is different.

Play alone

Something to play every day on your own, against the bots. Everything you unlock, you unlock by playing: there's no shop.

The daily show

One show a day, the same for everyone, and one go at it. You get a card at the end to post, with your streak on it.

Festival Week

The festival week of NEXT: Valletta, Monday 24 to Thursday 27 May, as four chapters. Thursday ends at the rooftop pool, and you have to win it.

Time trials

The six races on your own, against a ghost of your best run. Bronze, silver and gold for each.

Your look

Lanyards, the tote bag, booth socks, the espresso martini hat and more. Other players see what you're wearing online.

The rounds

A show is five random rounds and one winner. Or pick any round from the menu and play it on its own.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There's nothing to buy and no account to make.

Which computers does it run on?

Any laptop or desktop from the last few years, with a keyboard and a mouse or trackpad. It's made for Chrome and Edge: Safari and Firefox may work, but Chrome is where it's tested. The first visit loads about 22 MB; after that it opens quickly. If a laptop struggles, the game lowers its graphics by itself. Phones and tablets aren't supported yet.

Do I need to sign up for anything?

No. You type your name and pick a colour the first time you play. Your name, your Festival Week progress, your daily show streak and your looks are kept in this browser on this computer. Another browser or computer starts fresh, and so does a private window.

What does the game send over the internet?

When you play online, your bean's moves, your chat and your voice (only while you hold V) go to the other players in your game, through the NEXT.io game server. Nothing else. The server passes it on and doesn't keep it.

How do I look around?

Click inside the game and it takes the mouse: moving it turns the camera. Esc gives you the cursor back and pauses (online, the show carries on).

Where do my photos and posts go?

Photo mode pictures, the post cards and your daily show cards download to your Downloads folder.

The controls?

WASD or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, Shift to dive, E to grab. A controller works too. F1 opens the settings.

Is there an app?

Yes, the same game for Mac and Windows, for anyone who'd rather install it. It plays online with the browser version. Ask the NEXT.io team for it.

Something's broken.

Tell us at NEXT: Valletta, or message the NEXT.io team. A screenshot helps a lot :)
